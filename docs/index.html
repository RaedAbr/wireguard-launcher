How it works

Four steps, no setup.

  1. ChoosePick a country and how long you need the VPN, from 15 minutes to 30 days.
  2. Confirm in AWSSign in to your AWS account, tick one box, and tap Create stack.
  3. Come backAfter about 3 minutes, open the Outputs tab and tap your link.
  4. ConnectAdd the configuration to the WireGuard app. You're done.

One VPN works on one device.

Each VPN you start is made for a single phone or computer. Don't use its configuration on a second device, because the two would keep disconnecting each other. Need a VPN on another device? Start a separate one from that device, in a different country.

Why it's different

It's yours, and then it's gone.

Only you use it

The server is in your AWS account, and nobody else's traffic shares it.

Your key stays with you

Your private key is made in this browser. This page has no server, and it's blocked from sending data anywhere.

It cleans up after itself

When your time is up, the VPN deletes itself. You can delete it sooner in AWS.

You check before anything happens

AWS shows you the whole template, and nothing is created until you confirm.

Questions

Good to know

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Do every step in the same phone browser, because that's where your private key is saved.

Why is there a box to tick in AWS?

The VPN includes a small role that lets it delete itself when your time is up. AWS asks you to agree whenever a template creates a role. The role has no password or access keys.

Can I pause a VPN?

No. A stopped server would get a new address and stop matching your configuration, and its disk would keep costing money. Delete it, then start a new one in about 3 minutes.

What if the VPN can't delete itself?

The server has a backup timer. One hour later it shuts down, and AWS removes the server and its disk, so the charges stop.

Why can't I see all my VPNs here?

This page can't read your AWS account. It lists the VPNs started in this browser. Your AWS CloudFormation page always shows them all.
